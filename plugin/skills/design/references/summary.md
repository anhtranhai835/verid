# Plain-language summary before the no-brain signature

In no-brain drive the person who signs with `verid order` may not be a developer, and
`research.md`, `spec.md` and `design.md` are written for builders. Before asking for the
signature, write `.verid/summary.md` so they can decide what they are agreeing to.

- One page, plain language: no jargon, code, file paths or requirement IDs.
- Say what will be built, what will not, the main choices made and why, and the risks or
  costs the signer accepts.
- List anything still open as a question they can answer in one line.
- Keep it in step with the documents: rewrite it whenever research, spec or design changes
  before signing.

Then point the signer to `.verid/summary.md` first, and to the three documents for detail.
